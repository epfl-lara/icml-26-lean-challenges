\begin{lemma}[isMIntegralCurveAt_iff']
Let $M$ be a manifold and $v$ be a vector field on $M$. Then $\Gamma : ℝ → M$ is an integral curve of $v$ at $t_o$ 
if and only if there exists an open neighborhood $U$ of $t_o$ such that $\Gamma$ is an integral curve of $v$ on $U$.
\end{lemma}
